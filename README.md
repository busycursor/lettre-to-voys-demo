# Lettre to Voys: demo

A read-only demo of the Read view: manuscript pages read aloud in a reconstructed period pronunciation. Each word lights on the page and in the text as it's spoken, with its gloss, its IPA and where that pronunciation comes from.

Texts (each has its own address):
- https://busycursor.github.io/lettre-to-voys-demo/#/read/prick-of-conscience
- https://busycursor.github.io/lettre-to-voys-demo/#/read/beowulf

- **Manuscript images:** the Houghton Library, Harvard University (terms: https://nrs.lib.harvard.edu/urn-3:hul.ois:hlviewerterms), and the British Library (Cotton MS Vitellius A XV), each credited under its page.
- **Pronunciations:** derived by cited sound rules: Kökeritz, *A Guide to Chaucer's Pronunciation* (1962), for Middle English; Hogg, *A Grammar of Old English* (1992), for Old English.
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs `eleven_v4`, from inline IPA.

This is a static snapshot: nothing here runs, voices or edits anything.
